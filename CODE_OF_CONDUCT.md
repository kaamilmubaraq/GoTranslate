# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, trolling, and sexualized
attention are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Project maintainers may remove or edit contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening,
or harmful. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as
possible.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
