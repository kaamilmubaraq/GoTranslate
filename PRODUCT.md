# GoTranslate

## Register

product

## Users

People working with Japanese documents who need readable translations and terminology.

## Product Purpose

Extract Japanese text from images and PDFs, translate complete documents, and export useful results. Preserve source text for comparison and retain vocabulary as a secondary workflow.

## Brand Personality

Professional, focused, approachable.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, study flashcards as the primary experience, and unsupported claims of translation accuracy.

## Design Principles

- Put the document and its translation first.
- Make processing state and incomplete results explicit.
- Keep source text available for review.
- Make useful actions easy to find.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
